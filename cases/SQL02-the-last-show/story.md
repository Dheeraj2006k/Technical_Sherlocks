# Hook

At the QUBIT closing performance, the final reveal should have featured the original **Starfall Mask**, a one-of-a-kind prop signed by the student performers who built the show. Instead, the audience saw a replica. After the curtain call, the original was gone from its stand.

The organisers initially treated it as a theft. But the backstage records are messy: several people had legitimate access, a performer had argued about the finale, the cue operator triggered the reveal, and the prop team used a backup during rehearsal. **Being nearby is not the same as moving the mask.**

Your team has access to seven SQL tables containing staff roles, access events, prop hand-offs, inventory checks, cue records and internal messages. Use joins and filters to answer the investigation questions. The case is designed for three investigators working together; all players share one team state and one score.

## Phase 1 briefing

Phase 1, The Empty Stand. Determine which item was expected at the finale, what was actually present, and which people had duties connected to it. Do not accuse someone just because their job gave them access.

## Phase 2 briefing

Phase 2, The Swap Window. Reconstruct the last confirmed handling of the original and compare it with the inventory check and cue records. A badge event proves a credential was used at a door; it does not prove who carried an object.

## Phase 3 briefing

Phase 3, The Cover Story. Use the movement records and messages to test whether the substitution was accidental, authorised or concealed.
