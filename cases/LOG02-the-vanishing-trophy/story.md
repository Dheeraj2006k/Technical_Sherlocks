# Hook

The QUBIT Founders' Trophy is due to be presented at the closing ceremony at 5:00 PM. At 4:25 PM, the awards team discovers that the trophy is missing from its display stand.

No broken glass is found. The display cabinet was opened several times during the afternoon for approved photographs and stage preparation. Several people handled nearby equipment, and more than one movement record has an incomplete destination.

The first report calls it a theft. But the records show that the trophy was moved during event preparation—and no one has yet established where it went afterward.

**Your task:** Reconstruct the trophy's chain of custody, test the competing explanations, and determine who was responsible.

## Phase 1 briefing

Phase 1, Discovery. The awards display checklist, the photograph contact sheet, the volunteer movement notes and the ceremony schedule are open in the Evidence Browser. Establish what was really verified and which movement record leaves a gap.

## Phase 2 briefing

Phase 2, Following the Object. The temporary movement slip, Naina's statement, the storage crate register, the engraving proof and Arjun's statement are open. Separate authorization from return and follow the object through the gaps.

## Phase 3 briefing

Phase 3, Reconstruction. The awards folder inventory, the storage area inspection, the revised ceremony draft, the vendor call record and the final location check are open. Combine custody, timing and motive without treating any one of them as proof.
