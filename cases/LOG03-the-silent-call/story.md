# Hook

At the QUBIT Innovation Showcase, a student team is scheduled to demonstrate a prototype called **Pulse** at 4:30 PM. At 4:12 PM, the stage coordinator receives a message that the faculty supervisor has cancelled the demonstration because of an electrical safety concern.

The demonstration is stopped. The audience is redirected, and Pulse is moved into a storage room while staff check the setup. At 4:35 PM, the faculty supervisor arrives and says she never cancelled the demonstration.

Several people insist that they called, received a call, or heard the cancellation confirmed. The phone records appear to disagree with their stories.

Your job is not simply to find who made a call. You must determine **which calls actually connected, what information was communicated, and who knowingly turned an unconfirmed message into an official cancellation**.

## Phase 1 briefing

Phase 1, The Silent Call. The stage running sheet, the control desk call log, Karthik's statement and the safety checklist are open in the Evidence Browser. Test the claimed 4:11 confirmation against what the logs actually record.

## Phase 2 briefing

Phase 2, Two Sides of the Call. The faculty office's own phone log, Sana's clarification, Karthik's message draft and Dev's call history are open. Compare the two independent systems and what was really said at 4:10.

## Phase 3 briefing

Phase 3, The Missing Confirmation. The control desk procedure card, the approved contact sheet, a private note recovered from the stage desk and the combined timeline are open. Decide what the rules required and what the note adds.
