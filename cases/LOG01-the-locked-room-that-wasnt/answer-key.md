# LOG01 The Locked Room That Wasn't: answer key (PRIVATE)

Responsible: **Vikram Sethi**

**Motive.** Aster had failed its rehearsal output check. Vikram feared the demonstration would collapse and that his failure to report the fault would be exposed, so he staged a theft to create a reason to postpone the demonstration.

**Method.** Vikram removed the working prototype from Innovation Room B at about 4:12 PM while it was still open, put the nonfunctional display replica in its place, carried the genuine device out in a fitted flat black equipment case from the AV booth, drafted a security-related delay at 4:14 PM and let the normal 4:20 PM closure go ahead.

**Explanation.** The 4:18 photograph already shows the replica, so the substitution happened before the room was closed and no one had to enter a locked room. Vikram received the failure warning at 4:01 PM, signed out the black case at 4:02, was seen carrying it at 4:08, drafted a security-incident delay at 4:14 before anyone reported a loss, and the returned case holds a fitted insert with tape matching Aster's packaging. No single record captures the removal, but together they converge on him.
