# Hook

At the annual college innovation showcase, a working prototype called **Aster** is due to be demonstrated at 4:45 PM. It is kept in a display case inside Innovation Room B.

At 4:30 PM, the display case is opened for a final check. The Aster prototype is gone. An object that looks similar remains in its place.

The room door is closed. The key register shows no unusual key issue. No one admits entering after the room was secured.

The event coordinator claims that someone must have entered a locked room without leaving evidence of entry.

**Your task:** Investigate the evidence. Was Aster stolen from a locked room, or did everyone make the wrong assumption about when it disappeared?

## Phase 1 briefing

Phase 1, The Locked Room. The closure log, the movement notes, two official photographs and the rehearsal note are open in the Evidence Browser. Test the idea that Aster was stolen from a locked room, and separate what the records say from what people assume.

## Phase 2 briefing

Phase 2, Investigation. The prototype identification sheet, the enlarged 4:18 photograph, Rohan's recorded account, the AV booth equipment register and the maintenance record are open. Find out which object was actually in the case and test each lead against an independent record.

## Phase 3 briefing

Phase 3, Reconstruction. The rehearsal follow-up message, the unsent schedule draft, the AV booth inspection, Aditya's identification and the witness timeline are open. Put every record in time order and choose the hypothesis with the fewest unsupported assumptions.
