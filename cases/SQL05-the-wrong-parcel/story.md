# Hook

**Your role:** Solo investigator.

At 4:10 PM, the QUBIT demo desk opens parcel **PX-41**. It contains spare stage clips—not the prototype sensor module expected by the team. The dashboard says **PX-14** was delivered, but nobody at the demo desk signed for it.

Three people had legitimate roles in the delivery chain:
- **Ravi Menon** — dispatch clerk who prints labels
- **Sana Qureshi** — courier handoff coordinator
- **Joel D'Souza** — demo desk receiver

A delivery scan records what the system was told, not necessarily what was physically inside a parcel. The label history, route scans, expected delivery and desk receipt may not tell the same story.

## Phase 1 briefing

Phase 1, The Mix-up. Compare what was expected at the Demo Desk with what was actually received. A delivery scan records what the system was told, not necessarily what was physically inside a parcel, so read the expectation and the receipt before anything else.

## Phase 2 briefing

Phase 2, The Route. Read the label audit and the route scans to see why the dashboard says delivered and who touched the labels. Ask whether a courier mistake could produce this pattern, or whether the changes were made on purpose.

## Phase 3 briefing

Phase 3, The Cover-up. Bring the audit, the scans and the messages together and decide what the full record supports. Be careful to claim only what the records show about where the module went.
