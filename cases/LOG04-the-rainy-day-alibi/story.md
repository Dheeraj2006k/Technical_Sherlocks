# Hook

At the QUBIT Innovation Showcase, the original signed judging summary for the **Aster** prototype category disappeared from the judges' office between 3:15 PM and 3:40 PM. The judges had already agreed on the winner, but the summary sheet contained the signed tie-break decision needed to publish the result. A replacement summary appeared in the event folder at 3:42 PM with a different tie-break note. The judges paused the announcement while the records were checked.

The office corridor had a single badge-controlled entrance. Several staff members had reasons to be nearby. One person says the heavy rain kept them at the covered loading bay throughout the relevant period. The weather records, access records and ordinary event paperwork do not all tell the same story.

Your task is to determine who replaced the summary, and which explanation best accounts for the evidence. Do not assume that being near a room proves someone entered it, or that one inconsistent statement automatically proves guilt.

## Phase 1 briefing

Phase 1, The Alibi. Four records are open in the Evidence Browser: the rainfall station, Devika's first statement, the corridor badge log and the original document handover. Decide what each one proves and, just as important, what it cannot prove.

## Phase 2 briefing

Phase 2, The Record Changes. The judges' folder inventory, the comparison of the two summaries, the printer audit and the stage rehearsal log are now open. Establish exactly what changed in the document and what the printer record does and does not show.

## Phase 3 briefing

Phase 3, The Contradiction. The badge custody check, the service ticket, the revision request, the judge's confirmation and the secretary's incident note are open. Weigh the independent records against one another; no single record is conclusive.
