# Hook

At 9:52 PM on the final night before QUBIT opens, Arjun Varma is found unconscious beside the damaged power-control cabinet for Pulse, the festival's interactive LED wall.

His teammate Nikhil Reddy had argued with him earlier. Nikhil's workshop entry appears close to Arjun's final entry, and Nikhil says he left before Arjun arrived.

But three systems recorded the night—and their clocks disagree.

Your team must identify who was responsible for what happened to Arjun, reconstruct the sequence of events, and distinguish knowing negligence from an intentional attack.

## Phase 1 briefing

Phase 1, Who Was Still There? Use the team roster, workshop access log, equipment checkouts, statements and the maintenance report to establish who was in or near the workshop after 9:20 PM. Block presence is not workshop presence.

## Phase 2 briefing

Phase 2, The Fault and the Alibi. Read who was told about the Module 04 fault and when, and test Nikhil’s account against independent device and activity records. A single statement is not an alibi.

## Phase 3 briefing

Phase 3, Seven Minutes Late. Correct the clocks of the power controller and the corridor camera against the workshop access log, then match events across systems and decide what the records do and do not prove.
