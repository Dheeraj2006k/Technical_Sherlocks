# Hook

QUBIT’s showcase is running on a tight schedule. Team Comet submitted a two-minute demo video before the deadline. At 4:20 PM, the video begins with the right title—but ends after just 42 seconds, skipping the reveal that judges were waiting for. The team’s slot is paused, and Team Orbit is next in line.

Your job is to work out how the wrong video reached the playback system, who was responsible, and whether it was an accident or a deliberate move. You do not need technical knowledge: treat filenames, times, checksums and handoff records as clues. A checksum is simply a file’s identifying fingerprint.

## Phase 1 briefing

Phase 1, The Cut. The judges' incident card, the approved run sheet, the upload kiosk receipt and the AV booth duty card are open. Establish what happened on stage and what Dev's submission does and does not prove.

## Phase 2 briefing

Phase 2, The File Trail. The sealed-drive handoff log, the playback device report, the workstation export history and Tara's label sheet are open. Follow the file from submission to playback and decide where the chain broke.

## Phase 3 briefing

Phase 3, The Final Export. The booth reflection photo, the rehearsal cut's end card, a cue draft and the kiosk archive comparison are open. Combine the file trail with the physical and written evidence without overclaiming.
