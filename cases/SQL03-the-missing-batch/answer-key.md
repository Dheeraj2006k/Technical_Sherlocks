# SQL03 The Missing Batch: answer key (PRIVATE)

Responsible: **Nisha Varma**

**Motive.** To avoid a visible disruption before the evening sponsor photo, she asked for the replacement to be treated as the original batch.

**Method.** She held B-17 in the logistics cage under her custody, requested the B-17R reprint on an approval reference that belonged to another order, and later said the original went to Kabir at 14:46.

**Explanation.** The official custody trail breaks while Nisha is the recorded custodian. The records prove deliberate diversion and a cover-up, not what happened to the physical badges afterwards.
