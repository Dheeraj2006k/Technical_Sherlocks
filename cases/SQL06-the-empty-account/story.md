# Hook

The morning before QUBIT opens, the QUBIT Prototype Fund dashboard shows **₹0 available**. The fund received ₹42,000 the previous day. Two supplier payments were ordinary. A third ₹12,000 reserve payment was rejected, reversed, and apparently sent again.

The logistics lead says the replacement was just a routine retry and the dashboard is behind on syncing. But the replacement settled, and the gateway records do not look identical.

Your job is to reconstruct the money trail, compare the approval with the replacement request, and identify who made the suspicious change. You can prove a destination changed; you cannot identify the real-world owner from a fingerprint alone.

## Phase 1 briefing

Phase 1, The Balance. Inspect the account snapshots and the ledger. Separate a dashboard that is out of sync from money that actually left the account.

## Phase 2 briefing

Phase 2, The Approval Trail. Compare the replacement payment request with the original approval and the people involved. An approval covers a payee, a purpose and an amount, so ask what exactly the approval authorised.

## Phase 3 briefing

Phase 3, The Gateway. Use the gateway fingerprints, the audit events and the reconciliation notes to test the “routine retry” explanation.
