# Hook

**Team:** Two investigators  
**Objective:** Work out who used a borrowed access badge to enter the equipment archive after hours, what they were actually trying to retrieve, and how the logs were made to point at someone else.

At 6:40 PM, a sealed envelope containing the final QUBIT demo-room access tokens is found missing from the equipment archive. The archive door log records badge **B-204** entering at 6:17 PM. B-204 belongs to **Ishaan Rao**, a student volunteer who says he returned it before 5:30 PM.

Four people had plausible reasons to be near the archive:

- **Ishaan Rao** — volunteer whose badge ID appears in the door log
- **Maya Kapoor** — inventory coordinator with archive access
- **Arjun Sen** — AV technician who needed a replacement cable
- **Pooja Nair** — demo-room coordinator responsible for the access-token envelope

A door log is not the same thing as proof of who physically carried a badge. You will need to connect badge checkout records, door events, equipment requests and the archive inventory trail.

## Phase 1 briefing

Phase 1, The Access Log. Establish what is missing from the archive and what the door log does and does not prove about who was carrying the badge.

## Phase 2 briefing

Phase 2, The Borrowed Identity. Follow badge B-204 from checkout to return and test the other people who had a plausible reason to be near the archive.

## Phase 3 briefing

Phase 3, What Was Really Taken? Compare the inventory notes and the messages with the custody trail and decide what the evidence supports.
