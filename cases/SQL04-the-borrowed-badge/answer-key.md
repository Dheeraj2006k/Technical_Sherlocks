# SQL04 The Borrowed Badge: answer key (PRIVATE)

Responsible: **Maya Kapoor**

**Motive.** To delay the demo-room handover after she found a token-list mismatch, and to deflect blame onto Ishaan.

**Method.** She took custody of Ishaan’s returned badge B-204 for a scan, entered the archive with it at 18:17, removed the access-token envelope, then amended the inventory note to say the return was unconfirmed.

**Explanation.** The evidence supports concealment and interference with the handover; it does not prove she meant to use the tokens.
